## SECTION 1 — OVERALL QUALITY AND ORIGINALITY

**Concern O1:** Manuscript self-describes as a working draft; source verification pending.  
**Response:** The revised manuscript is re-presented as a "revised submission" with a structured CONSORT-format abstract and an explicit, up-front statement that results are a provisional reanalysis of an unverified extract. Editorial notes E1–E11 enumerate every verification item. No claim of effectiveness or safety is made beyond recorded associations.

**Concern O2:** Trial registration number/registry/date not identified.  
**Response:** Declarations section now states registration status is unconfirmed and provides a placeholder for registry name, number, and date (Editorial Note E1). The author must register the trial (prospectively if any activity remains; otherwise disclose retrospective registration) before submission.

**Concern O3:** Protocol and prespecified statistical analysis plan unavailable.  
**Response:** Methods §2.2 (randomization), §2.8 (analysis), and Declarations now state that the protocol and SAP were not available and must be obtained (Editorial Note E2). The primary estimand is explicitly defined as the difference in recorded mean total operative time by recorded group.

**Concern O4:** Allocation concealment implementation unverifiable; envelope numbers reassigned by arm.  
**Response:** Methods §2.3 and Results §3.5 disclose that EnvelopeNo values were reassigned by arm (001–100 vs 101–200) and that no original opening-order subset is retained; the author is directed to obtain the allocation log (Editorial Note E3). Concealment is described as "appropriate in principle, unverified in implementation."

**Concern O5:** Blinding of participants, assessors, analysts unverified.  
**Response:** Methods §2.3 states surgeon blinding is impossible and masking of participants, assessors, and analysts is unverified; detection-bias risk is acknowledged (Limitation, §4.3).

**Concern O6:** Analysis not established as intention-to-treat; no prespecified analysis plan verification.  
**Response:** Methods §2.8 and Results §3.1 state the analysis is by recorded group and is not presented as a verified ITT analysis; per-protocol and as-treated sensitivity analyses are deferred pending allocation-log verification (E2, E3).

**Concern O7:** Counts screened/excluded/randomized unavailable; no CONSORT flow.  
**Response:** Results §3.1 states a CONSORT 2025 flow diagram cannot be constructed and directs reconstruction from enrollment records (Editorial Note E6).

**Concern O8:** Unexplained cross-arm correlation (r = 0.993) with constant 10–18 min offset.  
**Response:** Results §3.3 and §3.6, Discussion §4.2 and §4.3 present this as a headline data-quality anomaly requiring source-document explanation before independence can be assumed (Editorial Note E8). Nominal CIs are not presented as confirmatory.

**Concern O9:** Conflicting earlier means (38.40 / 52.60 min) not reproduced.  
**Response:** Results §3.6 and Discussion §4.2 disclose the unreconciled discrepancy and require documentation of the file version analyzed (Editorial Note E7).

**Concern O10:** Device identity unverified ("Hemoclip" label vs Hem-o-Lok; "LT400-equivalent").  
**Response:** Nomenclature standardized as "titanium clip" vs "polymeric locking clip" with a standing footnote that the export label is "Hemoclip" and device identity (manufacturer, model, catalog number, applier, per-case counts) must be confirmed from operative records and device documentation (Editorial Note E4). Methods §2.4 specifies what must be verified.

**Concern O11:** Primary endpoint (total operative time) does not isolate clip effect.  
**Response:** Methods §2.5, Results §3.3, and Discussion §4.2 explicitly state that total operative time is not stump-closure time and that the device effect cannot be isolated; the primary estimand is defined accordingly.

**Concern O12:** Safety analysis underpowered; no noninferiority margin.  
**Response:** Methods §2.6 states no sample-size calculation was available, 13 total events substantially underpower safety analysis, and no noninferiority/equivalence margin was prespecified (reference [28]). Conclusion states safety equivalence is not established.

**Concern O13:** Follow-up window undefined; readmission origin unknown; out-of-hospital events potentially uncaptured.  
**Response:** Methods §2.5 and Results §3.4 disclose that ascertainment method, severity grading, observation windows, readmission counting origin (surgery vs discharge), and out-of-hospital capture require confirmation (Editorial Note E9).

**Concern O14:** Single surgeon/center; ten-year period; secular changes limit transferability.  
**Response:** Methods §2.1 and Discussion §4.3 acknowledge single-center/single-surgeon design, the 2017–2026 window, and limited generalizability (no inference to complicated appendicitis, late device events, or pediatric subgroups).

**Concern O15:** Recruitment end date disputed; extract contains no exact dates.  
**Response:** Methods §2.1 and Results §3.6 state exact dates are unavailable and must be reconciled from earlier documents (Editorial Note E5); the title's "10-Year" descriptor was removed because the designation refers to the study period, not ten years of observation per participant.

**Concern: originality low; conflict with Uçar & Bulut (2024) near-null result unreconciled.**  
**Response:** Introduction §1 now discusses Uçar & Bulut [10] (53.1 vs 52.4 min) and explicitly notes the tension with the −13.44 min recorded contrast; Discussion §4.2 makes the reconciliation a condition for clinical interpretation.
